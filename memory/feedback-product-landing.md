# Show the product and its identity together

## Why

A brand-consistent landing can still undersell a product when it covers only a small part of the implemented capabilities and lacks imagery connected to the brand's origin.

## How to apply

Before redesigning a product landing, audit the implemented features and map each public claim to evidence. Preserve the visual identity the user likes, add original subject-specific imagery, and demonstrate meaningful features with concrete examples. State price, source availability and release limitations clearly. Take inspiration from reference sites without copying their artwork, compositions or unsupported claims.

# Audit owner identity beyond visible branding

## Why

A rebrand can leave the previous maintainer's persona in demo notes, tests, slugs, example emails and generated snapshots even after the product name and icon change. Hugo requested the display identity Hugo Dória throughout Métis.

## How to apply

Use Hugo Dória for the current project's owner and matching example personas, with hugo-doria for machine identifiers and example.com for fictional contact data. Audit tracked source, filenames, fixtures, docs, packaged resources and the deployed site. Update references and UTF-8 byte-count expectations together. Preserve factual provenance and historical approvals without transferring them to the new maintainer; link original policies without presenting edited text as a verbatim original. Leave dependency authors and unrelated people intact. Do not rewrite Git history.
